Craftsmanship & Industrial Scale

Where Industrial Capacity Meets Artisan Detail

Founded with a commitment to uncompromised textile quality, MS Printing bridges the gap between massive manufacturing capability and the meticulous standards of independent streetwear brands, hospitality companies, and commercial enterprises.

Our state-of-the-art facility pairs computerized multi-color carousel automatic presses with master craft technicians who mix every ink by hand. We verify mesh tension, squeegee pressure, and radiant heat curing curves for every single batch to ensure colors stay vibrant through hundreds of washes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about minimum order quantities, artwork preparation, turnaround schedules, and payment terms.

Our standard minimum order for custom screen printing and embroidery is 24 pieces per design. For Direct-to-Garment (DTG) full-color prints or prototyping, we can accommodate smaller trial orders. Volume wholesale discounts automatically scale at 50, 100, 250, 500, and 1,000+ units.
We recommend vector formats: Adobe Illustrator (.AI), Vector PDF, EPS, or SVG with all fonts converted to outlines. We also accept high-resolution raster files (PSD, TIFF, PNG) with transparent backgrounds rendered at 300 DPI at actual print size. Our pre-press department provides complimentary artwork verification.
Standard production is typically 5 to 7 business days following digital proof approval and blank check-in. If you have an urgent deadline for an event or concert tour, we offer expedited 24-hour and 48-hour rush production services subject to press schedule availability.
Yes! You can mix garment sizes (XS through 4XL) and even mix garment colors, as long as the print graphic, print size, and ink color remain identical across the entire run. If ink colors need to change (e.g., printing black ink on white shirts and white ink on black shirts), a nominal color-change fee applies.
If your brand identity uses specific Pantone Solid Coated (PMS C) formulas, our master ink technicians hand-mix using precision digital scales to match your exact color formula within an optical tolerance of Delta E < 1.0.
Yes, we provide full retail finishing services! This includes manufacturer tag removal, 1-color screen printed interior neck tags, custom woven label sewing, size-sticker application, robotic fold-and-polybag, and barcode hang-tag attachment.
